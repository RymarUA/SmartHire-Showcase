FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about SmartHire.

How does the 14-day trial work?

Start with full Professional features for 14 days. No credit card required. Convert to a paid plan or switch to the free Basic tier when the trial ends.

What payment methods do you accept?

Payments run through WayForPay and LiqPay — cards and local Ukrainian payment methods. Money lands directly in your account.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely. Industry-standard encryption, regular backups, and strict per-tenant isolation — your data is never shared between businesses.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time. You'll retain access until the end of your billing period.
